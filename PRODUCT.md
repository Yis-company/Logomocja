# Logomocja
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->
## Platform
web
## Stack
delegated: vanilla JavaScript, Vite, Canvas 2D with software 3D projection. All dependencies bundled; no runtime services.
## Users
Polish-speaking learners exploring Logo, on desktop and phone. Inferred: school learners and adults returning to a familiar creative environment.
## Product Purpose
Write, understand, run and share turtle programs in a plane and in space.
## Operating Context
Offline static application; drafts and challenge progress stored on the device. No account.
## Capabilities and Constraints
Complete dialect and thirteen verbatim programs from the product brief. Animated execution, stepping, editor diagnostics, 2D pan/zoom and 3D orbit/pan/zoom, reference, checked practice, files and permalinks. Interpreter limits are authoritative.
## Brand Commitments
Polish UI, name Logomocja, nostalgia with modern usability. No incumbent assets.
## Product Principles
- Make drawing and code visible together.
- Preserve learners’ work.
- Explain failures at their source.
- Keep the app independent of services.
## Accessibility & Inclusion
Keyboard controls, visible focus, responsive layout, system light/dark with override. Reduced-motion support.
