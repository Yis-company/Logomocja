# Logomocja
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->
## Platform
web
## Stack
Delegated by the brief. Vite, TypeScript and Canvas; static, local assets only.
## Users
Polish-speaking learners exploring programming through turtle geometry, on desktop and phone. Inferred: school-age learners and nostalgic adults.
## Product Purpose
Write, understand, run and share Logo programs in flat and spatial geometry.
## Capabilities and Constraints
Full dialect and thirteen verbatim examples from the supplied brief. Local persistence, challenges, reference, files, sharing, keyboard access, light/dark themes. No backend, accounts or runtime network. Static subpath deployment.
## Brand Commitments
Polish interface, classic Logo nostalgia with modern usability. All further design decisions delegated explicitly by user.
## Product Principles
Drawing is immediate feedback. Errors teach. Drafts survive exploration. Geometry stays truthful.
