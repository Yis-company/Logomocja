# Logomocja

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Stack
delegated: TypeScript, Vite and Canvas 2D. A local mathematical 3D renderer keeps spatial drawing available without WebGL. Bun installs, builds and tests; output is static.

## Users
Confirmed: Polish-speaking learners exploring Logo, on desktop and phones. Inferred: a learner benefits from a visible example at first launch and short contextual teaching tips.

## Product Purpose
An offline turtle programming studio where learners read, run, modify, solve and share programs in a complete Polish Logo dialect.

## Operating Context
No accounts, backend or runtime network. Drafts and challenge progress live in local storage. Programs can be opened as files or encoded in URLs. The benchmark delegates all design and implementation decisions and explicitly excludes interviews and external services.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Editor with token highlighting and precise errors; animated execution and stepping; flat and spatial camera controls; thirteen verbatim examples; five automatically checked challenges; full reference; files, PNG and permalinks; themes and keyboard access. Respect the supplied interpreter semantics, limits, BASE_PATH and window.logomocja contract.

## Brand Commitments
Name: Logomocja. Polish interface. Nostalgia for the classic learning environment, with contemporary usability. No incumbent assets or visual system.

## Product Principles
- Make the link between a command and its drawing visible.
- Preserve the learner's work when exploring.
- Report errors precisely and in Polish.
- Keep all capabilities available without a connection.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Keyboard-operable labelled controls, visible focus, light/dark themes, reduced motion, responsive mobile workspace.
